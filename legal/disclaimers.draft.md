# Disclaimers (DRAFT)

> **Working draft, not legal advice.** Review with a solicitor before relying on it. These are the disclaimers the product should surface, especially near salary, financial, and career content.

## Guidance, not advice

Career Navigator provides general information and guidance to help you think about career options. It does not provide personal financial advice, careers advice regulated by any professional body, or a recommendation to pursue any particular path. Decisions about your education, career, or finances are your own, and you should seek qualified professional advice for your specific circumstances.

## About the numbers

The tool distinguishes between two kinds of information, and this distinction is shown in the app:

- **Salary figures and tax calculations** are based on published data (ONS and HMRC) but are estimates and general illustrations, not a promise of what any individual will earn. Take-home figures assume England and the stated tax year, and do not account for pensions, student loans, benefits, or personal circumstances.
- **The scores that drive matching and verdicts** (including the automation-risk score) are structured judgement, not measured data. The automation-risk score in particular is a forecast and should be read as one view, not a prediction.

## Route information

The described routes into each career are indicative of the typical path. Exact requirements vary by employer, by nation of the UK, and over time. Always confirm current requirements with the relevant professional body, awarding organisation, or regulator before making decisions.

## The CV feature

The CV analysis is an automated heuristic. It reads keywords and structure, not meaning, and can be wrong. It is a prompt for reflection, not an assessment of you or a substitute for professional review.
