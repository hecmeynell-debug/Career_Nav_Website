## What this is

Career Navigator is a careers guidance tool. This policy explains what data it handles and how.

## The short version

In the current prototype, the app runs in your browser. It does not have user accounts, does not store your data on a server, and does not track you. If you use the CV feature, your CV is read and analysed entirely on your device and is never uploaded.

## Data we process

**Your questionnaire answers and comparisons.** Held in your browser for the length of your session only. They are not sent anywhere and are lost when you close the tab.

**Your CV (if you use that feature).** Read and processed in your browser. The file itself is never sent to us or to any third party. To read PDF and Word files, the app loads a parsing library from a public code network (cdnjs); this downloads the library to your browser but does not send your file anywhere.
