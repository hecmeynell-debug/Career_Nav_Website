About these numbers, read me

Two tiers of trust. Salary figures and the take-home tax engine are anchored in UK market and HMRC data. The seven 0-10 axis scores, and every match and verdict, are structured judgement, not measured data; the future axis is a forecast about AI. Route maps are indicative of the typical path and vary by employer and UK nation; always check the professional body before committing. The work-style (RIASEC) profiles are a light heuristic; the MBTI compatibility is included because it's popular, but note MBTI is psychometrically weak (poor test-retest reliability, doesn't predict job performance), treat it as a conversation-starter, not a verdict. The external links are auto-generated UK search starting points.

CV & pivot

Paste your CV to see how it reads, how close you are to any career, and the smartest moves from where you are now, fused with your questionnaire once you've taken it.

Private by design. Your CV never leaves your browser and is never stored. PDF and .docx files are parsed on your device, only the small parser library is fetched from a CDN, never your file. This is a transparent heuristic that reads keywords and structure, not meaning, so treat it as a rough compass, not a recruiter's verdict. The meaning-aware version needs a server and is a deliberate later step.

From prototype to product

120 careers is a strong prototype library; a public build would push to hundreds, each with independently-sourced, cited salary and route data verified against the professional bodies, plus crowd-calibrated axis scores rather than one person's judgement, accounts to save results, and hosting. The honesty layer stays load-bearing, it's what separates this from the confident-but-hollow quizzes already out there.
