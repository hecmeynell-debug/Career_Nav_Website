CV & pivot

Paste your CV to see how it reads, how close you are to any career, and the smartest moves from where you are now, fused with your questionnaire once you've taken it.

Before you paste: leave out what is not needed. The analyser only looks for skills, qualifications and dates. It has no use for your address, phone number, email, date of birth, photo, national insurance number or referees, so delete those first.

Where it runs. Everything happens in this browser tab. Your CV is not uploaded, not stored, and not sent to any server or API, including when you upload a PDF or .docx: only the parser library is fetched from a CDN, never your file. Closing the tab discards it. There is no "clear" needed for a server because nothing reaches one, but the button below empties the box and the results anyway.

What it actually is. A rules-based keyword matcher, not an AI and not a recruiter. It looks up terms from a fixed dictionary and counts structural signals like dated roles and section headings. It does not read meaning, so it will miss anything phrased unusually and cannot judge whether your experience is any good. A meaning-aware version needs a server and is a deliberate later step.

From prototype to product

120 careers is a strong prototype library; a public build would push to hundreds, each with independently-sourced, cited salary and route data verified against the professional bodies, plus crowd-calibrated axis scores rather than one person's judgement, accounts to save results, and hosting.
